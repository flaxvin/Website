Three criteria behind every choice

The interesting technology should be in your product, not your infrastructure.

  1. Boring beats clever

    We pick the option with the largest hiring pool and the longest support horizon unless there is a concrete reason not to.

  2. Your team has to maintain it

    If a choice means only we can maintain the system, it is the wrong choice. Stack decisions are made with your hiring reality in mind.

  3. Decisions get written down

    Significant architecture choices are recorded as short decision records: what we chose, what we rejected, and why. Six months later, nobody has to guess.
