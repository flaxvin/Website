Six stages that surface risk early

Every stage below has an output you can read, click or run.

Discovery through to support

A week without a demonstrable output is treated as a delivery risk and escalated accordingly.

  1. 01

    Discovery and definition

    Week 0 to 2

    We sit with the people who will use the thing. Workflows get mapped, constraints get written down, and assumptions get separated from facts.

  2. 02

    Architecture and design

    Week 2 to 5

    Before anyone writes production code, we agree what we are building and how it fits together, including the states and edge cases that usually get discovered late.

  3. 03

    Build in two-week sprints

    Ongoing

    Working software every fortnight, demoed to you, deployed to an environment you can click through. No six-week silences.

  4. 04

    Test and harden

    Continuous

    Quality is built in per sprint, not bolted on at the end. Automated coverage grows with the codebase, and we test the unhappy paths deliberately.

  5. 05

    Deploy and launch

    At release

    Releases should be boring. Infrastructure is code, deploys are automated and reversible, and someone is watching the dashboards on launch day.

  6. 06

    Support and evolve

    Post-launch

    The build ends; the product does not. We continue under defined response times, or hand over to your team with documentation maintained to current state.

  • Monthly report and quarterly roadmap review
