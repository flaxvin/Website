Seven sectors we already know

Less of your budget goes on teaching us the basics.

The sectors and the usual work

We are not sector-exclusive. In these seven we already know the regulations, the integrations and the failure modes.

Fintech and financial services

Lending workflows, collections, reconciliation engines, KYC journeys and partner integrations, built with audit trails, data residency and RBI-era compliance expectations in mind from the first sprint.

  • Payment and UPI rail integrations
  • KYC / AML workflow automation
  • Reconciliation and ledger tooling
  • PCI-aware architecture reviews

Healthtech

Patient-facing apps, clinic operations, diagnostics workflows and interoperability work, handled with the privacy discipline that health data requires.

  • HIPAA / ABDM-aware data handling
  • Appointment and care-pathway systems
  • HL7 / FHIR interoperability
  • Consent and access auditing

Edtech

Learning platforms, assessment engines and cohort tooling that survive enrolment spikes and hold up on low-bandwidth connections.

  • Course delivery and LMS platforms
  • Proctoring and assessment engines
  • Offline-tolerant mobile learning
  • Learning analytics dashboards

Retail and e-commerce

Storefronts, catalogue and pricing systems, omnichannel inventory and the integration layer between your store, ERP, marketplace and 3PL.

  • Headless commerce builds
  • Inventory and order orchestration
  • Marketplace and POS integrations
  • Personalisation and search

Logistics and supply chain

Fleet, warehouse and last-mile systems with the offline behaviour, route logic and event tracking that field operations actually need.

  • Driver and field-ops mobile apps
  • Route planning and dispatch
  • Warehouse and WMS integrations
  • Real-time tracking and ETAs

SaaS and product companies

Product engineering pods for founders and product leaders: feature delivery, multi-tenancy, billing and the scale work that arrives with growth.

  • MVP to production in weeks
  • Multi-tenant architecture
  • Usage metering and billing
  • Performance and scale hardening

Manufacturing and industrial

Plant-floor visibility, quality tracking, maintenance scheduling and the bridges between shop-floor machines and back-office systems.

  • Production and OEE dashboards
  • IoT and sensor data pipelines
  • Quality and compliance tracking
  • ERP and MES integration

What domain experience changes

  • Compliance is a design input

    Data residency, audit trails, consent and retention get designed in during architecture, not retrofitted after a security review. It is considerably cheaper that way.

  • We ask your operators, not just your managers

    The person performing the task at 11pm on a Friday holds knowledge the requirements document does not capture. Discovery includes them.

  • Integration is usually the real project

    In most sectors the hard part is not the new system; it is making it agree with the six that already exist. We plan for that from the first estimate.

  • Outside your sector? Still talk to us

    If the domain is new to us we will say so, and price a longer discovery instead of pretending otherwise.

Operating in one of these sectors?

Tell us the problem in your own vocabulary. If we have solved something close to it before, we will show you what we learned.
